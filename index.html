Independent Research

I'm a student in the Todd Allen Phillips Center for Medical Sciences at Mills E. Godwin High School, completing an Independent Research Project (IRP) under the mentorship of Dr. Jayasimha Atulasimha at VCU. The project explores whether combining Liquid Neural Networks (which adapt to noisy, continuous time-series data) with Physics-Informed Neural Networks (which perform well under data scarcity) can improve sepsis detection on the MIMIC-IV Demo dataset — only 3 of 206 patients have sepsis.

Physics-Informed Liquid Neural Networks for Sepsis Classification

Mentored by Dr. Jayasimha Atulasimha, Virginia Commonwealth University In Progress — Fall 2026

Baseline Result (Liquid Neural Network)

Trained across 100 random seeds to isolate signal from noise. Mean AUPRC: ~0.154 vs. random baseline of ~0.024 (approximately 545% improvement). The true positive case was never missed across any seed.

Next Steps

Building PINN and PILNN variants. Potentially grounding the physics term in magneto-ionic transport equations as a physiological analog to mitochondrial electron transport chain dynamics during septic shock.

Applied AI

Clintelligence

A two-step "parse-then-analyze" diagnostic pipeline. Unstructured patient files are converted to structured JSON via few-shot prompting, then analyzed by a persona-prompted model — either Gemma 3n (on-device, private) or Gemini 2.5 Pro (cloud with search). Always includes a disclaimer to consult a real professional. Built with a Gradio interface to let users choose their model based on privacy needs.

Evaluation Infrastructure

M.C.A.I.T.

Medical & Cross-Disciplinary A.I. Test. A ten-task benchmark for rigorously evaluating LLMs in medical contexts: foundational biology (USABO questions), rare-disease diagnosis (ITP), common-disease differentiation (pneumonia), refusal under adversarial prompts, multi-turn differential diagnosis, implicit bias, X-ray multimodal diagnosis, prescription quality, mental-health diagnosis, and patient interaction. Current leaderboard leaders score 0.92–0.94.

Reinforcement Learning

AlphaCricket

A from-scratch RL pipeline (no pre-built environments). Custom PyMunk physics ragdoll paired with a PyTorch policy network and lightweight advantage-weighted regression update rule. Through continuous self-play, the agent learned swing mechanics to achieve a rolling average of 5.1 runs per ball against randomized deliveries.
