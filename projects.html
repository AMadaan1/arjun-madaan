MCAIT — Medical and Cross-disciplinary AI Test

Medical language models are usually scored on multiple-choice exam questions. That measures recall, not whether a model is safe to put in front of a patient. MCAIT is a ten-task suite that tries to measure the second thing: diagnostic reasoning, scientific knowledge, ethical judgement, and patient interaction, including agentic simulations where the model has to conduct a conversation rather than answer a prompt.

I designed the task set and scoring, implemented the harness, and ran it across 37 models. The full suite is published as public Kaggle notebooks so the runs can be reproduced and the scoring inspected.

Stanford AI4ALL

Vision transformers vs. convolutional networks for melanoma detection

Work in the medical AI research group at Stanford AI4ALL, run by the Institute for Human-Centered AI and the Stanford AI Lab. I compared how vision transformer and CNN architectures behave on melanoma classification — where the two families differ on limited, class-imbalanced dermatological image data, and what that implies about deploying either one in a screening setting.
